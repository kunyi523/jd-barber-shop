About

George, on Ottawa Street

George is the barber. The Ottawa Street BIA says he has over 49 years in the chair. He started at fourteen — sweeping the floor and watching his father’s work — then practised on family and friends, then hairdresser school. He trained in women’s and men’s styles. These days he specializes in men’s cuts.

He moved to Canada and settled in Hamilton after visiting friends and finding a strong Italian community here. The shop was at Eastgate for three years, then Main Street for twenty, then Ottawa Street North — a more visible spot on a street he already knew had a trendy history.

Walk-ins are accepted, or call 289-684-5758 to make an appointment. There is no online booking and no payments on this page.
